# Healthcare Analytics Dashboard – Business Insights

## 1. Overview

This document highlights the key analytical areas covered by the Healthcare Analytics Dashboard.

## 2. Revenue Analysis

- Monitor overall healthcare revenue.
- Analyse revenue trends over time.
- Identify doctors contributing to revenue performance.

## 3. Patient Admission Analysis

- Track total patient admissions.
- Analyse admission trends year over year.
- Compare different admission types.

## 4. Patient Demographics

- Analyse patient distribution by gender.
- Understand patient age group distribution.
- Examine patient blood group distribution.

## 5. Medical Analysis

- Analyse patient distribution by medical condition.
- Monitor medication distribution.
- Review patient test results.

## 6. Healthcare Provider Analysis

- Analyse hospital statistics.
- Review doctor performance.
- Compare insurance provider distribution.

## 7. Dashboard Value

The dashboard provides interactive insights into healthcare operations, patient admissions and financial performance, supporting data-driven analysis and decision-making.

## 8. Note

Detailed numerical findings and business recommendations will be documented after validating the dashboard measures and underlying dataset.
