# Healthcare Analytics Dashboard – Business Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

The Healthcare Analytics Dashboard is developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyse healthcare operations, patient admissions and financial performance.

## 2. Business Objectives

- Monitor total healthcare revenue.
- Track patient admissions over time.
- Analyse hospital and doctor statistics.
- Understand patient demographics.
- Analyse medical conditions and blood groups.
- Monitor insurance providers and medication distribution.
- Evaluate admission types and patient test results.
- Identify the top 5 doctors based on revenue and patient count.

## 3. Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – Overview

- Total Revenue
- Total Admissions
- Total Hospitals
- Total Doctors
- Blood Group Distribution
- Medical Condition Analysis
- Insurance Provider Analysis
- Medication Analysis
- Gender Distribution
- Admission Type
- Test Results
- Age Bucket Distribution
- Admission Trend YOY

### Page 2 – Financial Analysis

- Total Revenue
- Total Admissions
- Total Hospitals
- Total Doctors
- Patient Distribution by Blood Group
- Patient Distribution by Medical Condition
- Admission Trend YOY
- Top 5 Doctors by Revenue and Patients

## 4. Interactive Filters

- Doctor
- Hospital
- Insurance Provider
- Age Bucket
- Date of Admission

## 5. Expected Outcome

Provide an interactive Power BI reporting solution that helps users analyse healthcare operational and financial information through visual reports.
